Privacy Policy for المیقات (Al-Meeqat)

Last updated: October 16, 2026 100% Ad-Free & Private

Al-Meeqat (المیقات) - Islamic Astronomical Prayer Times & Qibla Suite ("we", "our", or "the app") is committed to protecting your privacy. This policy explains how your information is handled when using our mobile and web applications.

1. Information Collection & Usage

A. Location Data (GPS & Network Geolocation)

We access approximate and precise geographic coordinates (Latitude, Longitude, and Altitude) exclusively to calculate astronomically accurate Islamic prayer times and to determine the Great-Circle bearing towards the Holy Kaaba in Makkah. Location data is processed locally on your device, is never transmitted to any remote advertising or tracking server, and is never sold.

B. Device Sensors (Compass & Magnetometer)

Compass and geomagnetic field sensors are accessed in real time solely to point the Qibla Compass needle accurately towards Makkah. No sensor data is stored or uploaded.

C. Audio & Notifications

Audio playback is used to play the Adhan notification at your designated prayer times.

D. Local Storage & Export

Local app storage (SharedPreferences) is used to persist your chosen prayer calculation preferences, default city, and mosque schedules, and to allow you to export PDF/CSV timetable files to your device.

2. Third-Party Tracking & Ads

Al-Meeqat contains NO third-party advertising SDKs and NO behavioral analytics trackers.
