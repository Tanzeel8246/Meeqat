# Privacy Policy for المیقات (Al-Meeqat)
**Last Updated:** October 16, 2026

**Al-Meeqat (المیقات) - Islamic Astronomical Prayer Times & Qibla Suite** ("we", "our", or "the app") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how your information is handled when you use our mobile and web applications.

---

### 1. Information Collection & Usage

#### A. Location Data (GPS & Network Geolocation)
- **What We Access:** Approximate and precise geographic coordinates (Latitude, Longitude, and Altitude).
- **Why We Need It:** To calculate 100% astronomically accurate Islamic prayer times (Fajr, Sunrise, Ishraq, Nisf-un-Nahar, Zawal, Dhuhr, Asr, Maghrib, Isha, Tahajjud) and to determine the Great-Circle bearing towards the Holy Kaaba in Makkah (Qibla).
- **How It Is Handled:** Location processing is performed **locally on your device**. Your precise coordinates are **never transmitted to any third-party tracking server**, are **never sold to advertisers**, and are **never used for behavioral profiling**.

#### B. Device Sensors (Magnetometer & Accelerometer)
- **What We Access:** Compass (geomagnetic field) and motion sensors.
- **Why We Need It:** To dynamically rotate the Qibla Compass needle in real-time as you move and align your device towards the Kaaba. Sensor data is processed in real-time on-device and is never recorded or transmitted.

#### C. Audio & Alarms
- **What We Access:** Audio playback capabilities.
- **Why We Need It:** To play user-selected Adhan audio notifications at designated prayer times.

#### D. Local Storage & File Export
- **What We Access:** Local device app storage.
- **Why We Need It:** To persist your chosen settings (such as calculation method, default city, and mosque Jama'at schedules) and to allow you to save exported PDF, CSV, and HTML prayer timetable documents to your device downloads.

---

### 2. Third-Party Services
Meeqat does NOT integrate third-party advertising SDKs (e.g., Google AdMob, Unity Ads, Meta Audience Network) or user-tracking analytics (e.g., Firebase Analytics, AppsFlyer).

For optional online city geocoding, queries are processed via secure public geocoding APIs (such as Open-Meteo and OpenStreetMap Nominatim) solely to resolve city names to coordinates. No personally identifiable information (PII) is attached to these search queries.

---

### 3. Data Retention & Security
All user preferences and saved locations reside solely within your device's sandboxed local application storage (`SharedPreferences`). You can erase all data at any time by clearing the application cache/data in Android Settings or by uninstalling the application.

---

### 4. Children’s Privacy
Meeqat is suitable for users of all ages. We do not knowingly collect any personal identifiable information from children under the age of 13.

---

### 5. Changes to This Privacy Policy
We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect enhancements or legal requirements. Any modifications will be posted here with an updated "Last Updated" date.
